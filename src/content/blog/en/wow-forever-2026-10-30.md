Build 70707 (October 30) came with no official post that day. The changes below come from the wclbox build comparison (machine-translated from the Chinese client), and the names are checked against Blizzard's November 2 development notes, which cover this round in full. See the November 2 post for the official explanations and for everything that did not show up in the comparison.

## Character & Class changes

::class[Warrior]

- Protection: Revenge now deals 138 + 22.5% melee attack power to 168 + 22.5% melee attack power damage (was a flat 138 to 168), and still generates a lot of threat.

::class[Paladin]

- **Holy:**
  - :tip[Holy Power]{class=paladin} now increases the critical healing bonus of Holy spells by 50% at max rank (it used to raise critical strike chance).
  - :tip[Light's Vigil]{class=paladin} now has a 40 yard range and only works on allies; its damage effect is gone.
  - :tip[Holy Precision]{class=paladin} (Divine Precision) is down to 2 ranks and now also shortens Silence on you.
  - New talents: Oath of Light and Boundless Light. Voice of Truth, Blessing of Light and Greater Blessing of Light are removed.
  - :tip[Spiritual Focus], :tip[Improved Seals] and :tip[Reverence] change rank counts, and several talents move rows; :tip[Holy Conduit] moves from Retribution to Holy.
- **Protection:**
  - :tip[Righteous Fury] now raises all your threat by 33% and Holy threat by a further 90%.
  - :tip[Holy Shield]{class=paladin} drops to level 50 and 195 mana in the comparison. :tip[Swift Judgement]{class=paladin} now releases the Seal without sharing the Judgement cooldown.
  - :tip[Sacred Duty] no longer grants Stamina. :tip[Iron Creed] drops from 5 to 3 ranks, and :tip[Shield Specialization]{class=paladin} from 3 to 2.
  - New talents: Shieldsworn and Stoicism. One-Handed Weapon Specialization and Improved :tip[Seal of Fury] are removed.
- **Retribution:**
  - :tip[Conviction] now gives critical chance to all spells and attacks. :tip[Vindication] no longer raises attack power.
  - :tip[Two-Handed Weapon Specialization]{class=paladin} now also lowers threat while you wield a two-handed weapon.
  - :tip[Champion of the Light] goes to 5 ranks and shortens :tip[Hammer of Wrath]'s cast. Instrument of Law is removed.
  - New talents: Turn Evil and Unsealed Wrath (stored Seal copies, up to 3 stacks). Vengeance now requires :tip[Seal of Command]{class=paladin}.

::class[Hunter]

- The comparison lists higher numbers for :tip[Aspect of the Hawk], :tip[Serpent Sting], :tip[Arcane Shot], :tip[Aimed Shot], :tip[Trueshot Aura]{class=hunter}, :tip[Sniper Shot]{class=hunter} and :tip[Barrage] without naming the values. The exact figures are in the November 2 notes.
- Beast Mastery: :tip[Pathfinding] moves from row 2 to row 3.
- Survival: :tip[Lightning Reflexes]{class=hunter} is improved.

::class[Priest]

- Holy: :tip[Cure Disease]{class=priest} and :tip[Abolish Disease] can now be used in :tip[Shadowform]{class=priest}. Binding Heal is removed and replaced by Holy Binding (:tip[Flash Heal] also heals you for 50%, without threat).
- Shadow: :tip[Shadow Word: Death] is buffed.

::class[Mage]

- Arcane: :tip[Arcane Blast]{class=mage} is changed, :tip[Arcane Resilience] goes up to 3 ranks and :tip[Missile Barrage] is nerfed.
- Fire: :tip[Incinerate]{class=mage} is changed.
- Frost: :tip[Ice Lance]{class=mage} lists an 8 second cooldown; :tip[Ice Barrier]{class=mage} and :tip[Winter's Chill] are buffed.

::class[Warlock]

- :tip[Improved Drains] has a new tooltip.

::class[Druid]

- Balance: :tip[Genesis] is buffed.
- Feral:
  - :tip[Ferocious Bite] gets a new Rank 1 at level 24, and :tip[Lacerate]{class=druid} becomes a talent with new ranks learned at levels 25 and 34.
  - Primal Bite is now learned at level 60 in the comparison and moves to row 7.
  - Shifting Power's cooldown drops from 16 to 8 seconds.
  - :tip[Berserk] is removed. New talent Predatory Discipline. :tip[Enrage]{class=druid} and :tip[Heart of the Wild] change trees (:tip[Heart of the Wild] goes to Restoration).
- Restoration:
  - :tip[Wild Growth]{class=druid} is no longer instant; it has a 3 second cast.
  - :tip[Naturalist] now also shortens the cast time of a second spell and raises damage by 10% (was 5%).
  - Gift of the Earthmother is removed. New talent :tip[Bloom].

## Race changes

::race[Dwarf]

- Earth Fury is removed and Artisan's Precision is added (+1% hit chance with spells and attacks).

::race[Human]

- Ruthless is nerfed and Human Spirit is buffed.

::race[Orc]

- Savage is removed and :tip[Command] is added (Hunter and Warlock pets deal 5% more damage).

::race[Skyborne]

- Wind-Blessed Haste is nerfed for both Skyborne races.
- High Order: Read Leyline now casts in 0.5 seconds (was 2 seconds) and gives 50% regeneration for 15 seconds, with a 1 hour resonance bonus near a ley line.
- Windshaper: :tip[Skysight] gives 15% movement speed for 30 seconds, with a 1 hour recharge effect near an elemental convergence.

::race[Tauren]

- :tip[Cultivation] now lists a 15 minute cooldown.
- :tip[Plainsrunning] gives 2% speed per stack, up to 60%, and no longer affects mounts. Endurance only gives 5% Health.

::race[Troll]

- Berserking lasts 15 seconds (was 10).

::race[Undead]

- :tip[Touch of the Grave] internal cooldown is 15 seconds (was 25).

## New developments / system changes

- Many new or changed items appear in the build: Blacksmithing plans for chain shirts, leggings and helms; Leatherworking patterns for leather armor, pants and helms; the Heart of the Mountain enchanting formulas; Enchant Boots - Speed; and a set of Initiate's, Tracker's, Watcher's and Warden's Signets in four stat flavors.
- Starter consumables such as Roasted Quail, Minor and Lesser Healing and Mana Potions, a Woolen Bag and an Adventurer's Tent also appear.

## Known issues

- No official known-issues list came with this build.
