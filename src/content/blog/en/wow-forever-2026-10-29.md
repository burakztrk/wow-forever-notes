Between build 70685 and the next build, Blizzard published three official posts. None of them contains class, race or system changes; they are about the launch and onboarding.

## Launch date

World of Warcraft: Forever launches worldwide on November 4 at 3:00 pm PST. Blizzard describes a reimagined original Azeroth where you adventure to level 60 across the original continents, with new stories and a permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW, where community comes first.

## Guides for new and returning players

- **New Player's Starter Guide:** starts at level one, covers creating a character of your own and the essentials for finding your place in the world.
- **Old Player's Return Guide:** for players coming back after a few years or many, with the information needed to get back into the hero's journey.

The posts link to longer articles on Blizzard's site; this summary only covers the post introductions.
